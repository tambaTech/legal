Privacy Policy

FastingFit  ·  Last updated: October 15, 2026

Overview

FastingFit ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information FastingFit collects, how it is used, and your rights regarding your data.

Data We Collect

FastingFit stores the following data locally on your device: • Fasting session history (start time, duration, goal completion) • Nutrition logs (meals, calorie intake, macronutrient estimates) • Workout records (activity type, duration, calories burned) • Hydration logs • Body weight and personal health stats you enter manually • App preferences and settings This data never leaves your device except as described below.

Apple Health Integration

Apple Health is optional. With your permission, FastingFit can read these specific records: dietary energy consumed, body mass, height, body fat percentage, blood glucose, date of birth, workouts, step count, stand hours, active energy, resting energy, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, VO2 max, and sleep analysis. Nutrition and workout logging can write the types described in their permission prompts. Ask FastingFit's category-specific permission controls request read access only. FastingFit uses only records Health makes available; an empty result may mean no data or no read permission. Change access in Health → your profile → Apps → FastingFit, or disable sync in FastingFit Settings.

Private iCloud Record Sync

FastingFit uses your private iCloud account to sync persisted fasting sessions, meal records, saved meal-library items, and persisted app preferences. Workout records are deliberately excluded: they are imported from Apple Health, so they stay in a separate store on this device and are never written to iCloud. Apple handles iCloud storage under its privacy terms. Sync requires iCloud availability and may be delayed. Appearance and display preferences have a separate optional preference-sync switch in Settings → Health connections → Advanced; that switch does not disable record sync. Profile stats, journals, Health permissions, app lock, and other device-only settings are not included in record sync. Deleting synced records in FastingFit can propagate to other devices. Apple Health records are managed separately in Health.

Ask FastingFit and Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, Ask FastingFit uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models with recorded FastingFit history and optional readable Apple Health context. Health data is read for the requested topic and date range. FastingFit records, synced Health facts, and generated interpretations are identified separately. Generated answers are wellness information, not a medical diagnosis. Turn off Personalized Coaching in Settings to stop personalized AI generation; your records and standard tracking remain available. App Intents make supported FastingFit actions and summaries available to system experiences, with authentication for private summaries.

Prayer Times and Location Services

For calculated prayer times, FastingFit sends latitude and longitude for your chosen location, the requested date, time zone, and calculation settings to Aladhan (api.aladhan.com). Current Location uses coordinates made available by iOS; a city preset uses that city's coordinates. Choosing a city avoids sharing your device's current coordinates, but still sends the selected city's coordinates. City search and place lookup use Apple's location services. When you choose a myMasjid screen, FastingFit requests that screen's identifier and prayer timetable from time.my-masjid.com. These requests do not include your fasting history or Apple Health records. Providers can receive network information such as your IP address. Aladhan and myMasjid control their own request-log retention; FastingFit cannot promise immediate deletion. Change the source in Fasting → Prayer Times; revoke device location access in iOS Settings. Local solar calculation can be used as a fallback when Aladhan is unavailable; it is not a switch that disables online requests.

Online Food Lookup

Food lookup can send barcodes, food names or search text to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org) and to USDA FoodData Central (api.nal.usda.gov) to retrieve reference nutrition information. Open Food Facts is tried first and USDA second. Requests do not include your saved fasting history, profile, or Apple Health records. Both providers can receive ordinary network information, including your IP address, under their own privacy policies. Manual entry and the built-in meal suggestions remain alternatives to online lookup. Deleting a logged meal does not send a deletion request for a previous provider search.

On-Device Photo Recognition

Food photo recognition runs entirely on your device. The photo is never uploaded, never sent to a third party, and is not used to train anyone's model. You can also enter nutrition manually.

Backups and Reinstalling

Deleting FastingFit removes records stored in its app container. Fasting records that finished syncing can download after reinstalling with the same Apple Account and iCloud access. Restoration is not immediate and unsynced records cannot be recovered from iCloud. Profile stats are not automatically synced. You can explicitly save a fasting and profile backup file and restore it after reinstalling. This file contains personal information and is not encrypted by FastingFit; choose a secure location outside the FastingFit app folder and keep it private. FastingFit does not automatically upload these backups. Active timers, journals, meals, goals, and other settings are not included. Existing records and profile stats are kept when restoring.

Notifications

FastingFit may send local notifications for fasting milestones, hydration reminders, and goal alerts. These are generated on-device and do not involve any external servers. You can manage notification permissions in iOS Settings.

Data We Do Not Collect

FastingFit does not sell your personal information. Food photo recognition runs on your device and uploads nothing. We do not operate analytics servers, advertising networks, or crash-reporting services that receive your personal health data.

Children's Privacy

FastingFit is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected with an updated date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
